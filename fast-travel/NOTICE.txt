Fast Travel's original source is MIT licensed, copyright Clay Mullis.

src/program/ext/picojpeg.c and picojpeg.h are picojpeg v1.1 by Rich Geldreich, in the public domain.
Four colour-conversion writes of the form `*p++ = f(p[0])` were split into a write and an increment,
since their order is undefined in C.

The menu's text uses Inter (copyright The Inter Project Authors) and Cinzel (copyright The Cinzel Project Authors),
both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; the fonts and their licenses are in tools/fonts/. The
compiled module holds glyphs drawn from them.

The area pictures in assets/areas/ were made with an image generator from text descriptions
(assets/prompts.json); they are not taken from the game.

src/program/font8x8.hpp is font8x8_basic by Daniel Hepper, from the IBM PC BIOS font, in the public domain.

The v1.2.0 pchtxt builds with devkitPro/devkitA64 and uses imports from the game. The legacy subsdk5 builds include exlaunch, licensed under GPL-2.0. Upstream source: https://github.com/shadowninja108/exlaunch

The destination list contains area and spawn names and coordinates used to request warps. The game files and SDK are local build inputs.
